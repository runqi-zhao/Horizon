# Evaluation goal

Evaluate whether a shopping deal or buying recommendation is genuinely worth a reader's attention and money today.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Exceptional.** A verified price well below the usual market price for a desirable product, with clear purchase channel, stock, and conditions, or a buying guide that changes what a careful buyer would purchase.
- **7-8: Strong.** A meaningful discount or well-evidenced recommendation with enough product detail and conditions to act on, even if the price is not the historical low.
- **5-6: Worth knowing.** A modest discount, a niche product that suits only some readers, or a recommendation with limited price evidence.
- **3-4: Weak.** Small savings, unclear or unverifiable pricing, heavy affiliate framing, or a product with little practical advantage.
- **0-2: Noise.** Fake or inflated reference prices, expired promotions, pure advertising, or deals with no usable purchase information.

# Evaluation guidance

Judge the actual price against the product's normal street price, not the seller's crossed-out list price. Reward concrete model numbers, capacities, versions, included accessories, and verifiable conditions such as coupon thresholds, membership requirements, regional availability, and shipping. Penalize urgency language and "limited time" framing that is not backed by a date. A recommendation is only valuable when the evidence explains why this product at this price beats the alternatives a reader would otherwise consider.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
