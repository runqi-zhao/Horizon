# Deals and shopping profile

Use this profile for consumer purchase opportunities and shopping-oriented content: price drops, coupons, promotional events, buying guides, and hands-on product recommendations where the practical question is whether to buy now, what to buy, or how much to pay.

Typical items include a concrete product with a price and purchase channel, a limited-time promotion, a comparison that ends in a buying recommendation, or a community-tested deal report.

Do not use this profile for general technology industry news, product announcements without pricing or availability, pure brand marketing, affiliate spam without verifiable pricing, or long technical reviews whose main value is engineering analysis rather than a purchase decision.
